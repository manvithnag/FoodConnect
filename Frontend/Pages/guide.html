Guide and Food Safety

A short checklist so every meal we redistribute is safe to eat.

For Donors

Create an account, choose Donor, and post food in the dashboard. Include the food type, servings, pickup address, and collection deadline. Approve an organisation that can collect it in time.

For Organisations

Register as a recipient organisation, browse food listings, and request food you can distribute. Bring suitable containers and arrange collection at the agreed pickup time.

Food Safety

Donate only freshly prepared or properly sealed food. Keep it covered, record the preparation time, and never offer food that has already been served or has spoiled.

Does it cost anything?

FoodConnect is designed to support food redistribution. Check the relevant listing for any applicable arrangements.

How long does food stay safe?

Food safety depends on the type of food, preparation, storage temperature and handling. Follow applicable food-safety guidance and set a realistic collection deadline.

Can I donate from a small home function?

Yes. Individuals can share surplus food from birthdays, family functions and other events, provided it is safe to eat.

How do I earn a badge?

Completed donations can contribute to your total servings. Visit the Hall of Fame page to learn about donor recognition.
