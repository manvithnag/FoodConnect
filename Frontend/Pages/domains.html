Who can take part

FoodConnect has three kinds of accounts, each with its own login and tools.

Food providers (donors)

🎉

Individuals

Birthdays, weddings, festivals and family functions with food to spare.

🏨

Hotels

Buffet and banquet surplus at the end of service.

🍽️

Restaurants

Unsold, safe, freshly cooked meals.

🥘

Caterers

Extra food from large orders and events.

📋

Event organisers

Conferences, fairs and community events.

Food receivers (recipient organisations)

🏛️

NGOs and charities

Community kitchens and charitable trusts.

🧒

Orphanages

Meals for children in care.

👵

Old-age homes

Fresh food for elders.

🛕

Ashramas and shelters

Help for people without a meal.

FoodConnect is a non-profit food redistribution platform.

Reduce food waste. Increase social impact. Connect surplus food with those who need it most.
