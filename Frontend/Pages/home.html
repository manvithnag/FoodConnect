How FoodConnect works

Five simple steps from leftover food to a full stomach.

Donor posts food
Add what is available, how many it serves, and the pickup time.
Organisation browses
Verified NGOs and homes see live listings.
Organisation requests
One click sends a request to the donor.
Donor approves
The donor accepts the request that suits best.
Food is collected
It is picked up, served, and the donor earns recognition.
